# Entry 1 | Deciding on My Topic (Public Transportation)
Blog Entry | 10/19/2026

## Choosing my Topic
The topic I chose for this project is Public Transit, primarily as I've taken a deep interest in public transit infrastructure since I was a child and believe that I know this subject well enough to work on it. As a kid during the pandemic, being restricted to my home with my iPad, my interest was piqued at some point, likely when public transit footage came across my feed. Ever since then, I've slowly begun inching my way into the inner workings of public transit. Around 2022, I began going out in person to view public transit myself to gain a deeper understanding to how it all works and runs, and with almost five years of bystander experience in the field, I believe I can excel in working in this topic. From the very start of the subway system with the three independent competing companies, such as the [Independent Subway](https://www.nycsubway.org/wiki/History_of_the_Independent_Subway) and the [Brooklyn-Manhattan Transit Corporation](https://www.nycsubway.org/wiki/Chapter_3,_The_BMT), to modern infrastructure such as the [Second Avenue Subway Project](https://www.mta.info/project/second-avenue-subway-phase-2) and the [Brooklyn Bus Network Redesign](https://www.mta.info/project/brooklyn-bus-network-redesign), I specialize in any field of public transit, and I know what it takes to help improve commuter access for all. Being able to reimagine how public transit operates and innovates is quite the topic for me, and I know I can help theorize the future of public movement.

## Engineering Design Process
I'm now in the first stage of the engineering design progress, being defining the primary issue. My goal in fixing the various problems in New York City's transit system primarily revolves around speeding up service. For years, endless complaints have flooded every social media platform and conversation imaginable about the repeating delays and service disruptions faced by thousands of commuters every single day. My plan is to research this issue deeply, find its roots and define its various causes clearly, gain input from others on their experience in this, and then summarize it thoroughly with possible solutions to a problem that should not be plaguing such a massive transit system.

## Skills

#### Communication
Often, I prefer to avoid interacting with others in public, usually due to my nerves and to mind my own business. However, I will not get anywhere observing from a distance. My plan is to practice and gain more courage to ask others for their input on my project, as well as asking transit employees about how the transit system works more to accumulate notes to direct towards this project. In addition, I have deeply studied the work I have done in SEP10 so far, and I believe I need to ask questions to my teacher, Mr. Mueller as well to eliminate any potential confusion.
#### Deep Research
As I already know several transit employees personally and have observed the inner workings of the transit system the general public overlooks for years, I believe I know much more about how to improve the transit system. My plan is to ask transit employees I'm comfortable with with topics related to the subject, as well as to take pictures to use my own media to minimize copyright issues. Having firsthand experience and knowledge on a specific transit field allows me to have a deeper, more thorough understanding of the topic, therefore making for more realistic approaches and proposals.
